# Report pages

## 1. Executive overview
KPI cards for MRR, ARR, NRR, GRR, active accounts, ARPA and at-risk MRR; monthly MRR trend; MRR bridge; regional performance; operating-risk panel.

## 2. Revenue and MRR bridge
Opening-to-closing waterfall, movement trends, account-level movement table, plan and segment decomposition, constant-currency comparison.

## 3. Retention and cohorts
Logo and revenue retention, GRR/NRR trends, acquisition cohorts, churn by tenure, voluntary/involuntary churn and reactivation.

## 4. Sales and acquisition
Lead and opportunity funnel, win rate, sales cycle, channel and partner performance, country conversion, new MRR by motion.

## 5. Product adoption
Activation funnel, time to first value, seat utilisation, feature breadth, automation/API/integration adoption, dormant and under-utilised accounts.

## 6. Customer health and renewals
Health distribution, renewal pipeline, at-risk ARR, support and payment drivers, intervention coverage, account drill-through.

## 7. Billing and collections
Invoice volume and value, first-attempt payment success, retry recovery, dunning stages, overdue amounts, failures by method and country.

## 8. Experiments
Assignment and exposure integrity, primary and guardrail effects, confidence intervals, segment effects and explicit launch decision.

Global slicers: reporting month, region, country, segment, plan, billing cycle, sales motion and acquisition channel.
