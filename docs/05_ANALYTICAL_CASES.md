# Analytical Cases

The machine-readable catalog is `analytics/business_case_catalog.yaml`.

The SQL layer currently exposes 41 catalogued business cases across revenue, acquisition, product, customer success, billing, experimentation and executive reporting.

Each case includes:

- a business question;
- an analytical domain;
- a governed SQL view;
- the decision supported by the output.

The catalog prevents a common portfolio problem: producing many queries without explaining what management decision they support.
