# Limitations

- Data is synthetic and should not be interpreted as a benchmark for a specific company.
- Product events are sampled to keep local generation manageable; daily account aggregates preserve the analytical signal.
- MRR follows a commercial recurring-revenue definition, not statutory revenue recognition.
- CAC is based on attributed campaign spend and does not include every sales and overhead cost.
- Health score is deterministic business logic, not a predictive churn model.
- Experiment outcomes are generated under controlled assumptions and demonstrate evaluation workflow rather than real causal evidence.
- The repository provides a Power BI build specification and DAX library, not a binary `.pbix` file.
