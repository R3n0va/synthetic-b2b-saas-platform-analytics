# Business Context

## Product

The model represents a German-headquartered B2B SaaS company serving European field-service organisations. Customers use the platform to manage workspaces, field workers, schedules, work orders, documents, integrations and customer sign-off.

The core unit of analysis is the customer account. Each account can contain multiple workspaces, users, paid seats, subscriptions, add-ons, invoices and product events.

Local transaction currencies are converted to EUR with governed monthly FX rates. Country and commercial-region reporting remain available after conversion.

## Customer segments

- Micro: 1–5 paid seats
- Small: 6–20 paid seats
- Medium: 21–100 paid seats
- Mid-market: 101–500 paid seats
- Enterprise: more than 500 paid seats

## Sales motions

- Self-service: trial, card or SEPA payment, short conversion path.
- Sales-assisted: qualification, discovery, demonstration, proposal, negotiation and contract.
- Partner-led: reseller, system integrator or industry consultant.

## Pricing

The commercial model combines a plan base fee, paid seats and recurring add-ons. Monthly and annual billing are supported. Annual pricing includes a governed discount, while sales-assisted contracts can include an additional negotiated discount.

## Management questions

The project is designed around recurring management decisions rather than around isolated technical tasks:

1. Is recurring revenue growing through durable new business and expansion?
2. Which markets, segments and channels create customers with strong retention?
3. Where is paid capacity underused?
4. Which behaviours indicate adoption, expansion or renewal risk?
5. How much revenue is exposed to churn, failed payments or overdue invoices?
6. Which commercial, onboarding, product and customer-success changes should be launched?
